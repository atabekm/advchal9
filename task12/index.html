          One question, four people, at the same time.
          

            The grid with its question supplied by you, and one column of it that is
            not a person: no profile sends no block at all, so you can see
            what the same question gets when nobody has said who is asking. The four
            go out together and stream side by side — what differs between the columns
            is the entire contribution of the profile.
          

        

      


      

        
        

          
          
          
        

      


      

      

    

  


  
  

    

      

        

          The same questions, asked as four different people.
          

            Personalization is the part of the answer determined by who is asking rather
            than by what was asked. Hold the questions fixed, vary the person, and what
            changes is the whole of it. Two of these rows are not profiles:
            no profile is the baseline, and Sam again is the same
            request a second time — whatever differs there is the model disagreeing
            with itself, and it is the bar the other rows have to clear.
          

        

        

          
          
        

      

      

      

    

  


  
  

    

      

        

          Which fields is it actually taking into account?
          

            One profile, one field removed at a time, the same questions re-asked. A
            field whose removal changes nothing about the answer was never being taken
            into account — and it is paying for its tokens in the system block of every
            request, forever.
