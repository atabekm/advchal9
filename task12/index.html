          The same questions, asked as four different people.
          

            Personalization is the part of the answer determined by who is asking rather
            than by what was asked. Hold the questions fixed, vary the person, and what
            changes is the whole of it. Two of these rows are not profiles:
            no profile is the baseline, and Sam again is the same
            request a second time — whatever differs there is the model disagreeing
            with itself, and it is the bar the other rows have to clear.
