# Getting started with Flowdesk

Flowdesk is a task tracker for teams: projects, tasks with assignees and due dates, boards,
a calendar view, comments and attachments.

## Create an organization

Sign up at `flowdesk.app` with your work email. The first person becomes the organization's
**owner**. New organizations start on a 14-day Business trial; afterwards they continue on the
plan you choose, or on Free.

## Projects and tasks

A project holds tasks. Each task has a key (`OPS-142`), a title, an assignee, a due date,
a status and optional subtasks. Views: list, board (by status) and calendar. Recurring tasks
repeat daily, weekly, monthly or on the last working day of the month.

## Invite your team

**Admin › Members › Invite**. See [Members and invitations](members-and-invites.md).

## Notifications

Flowdesk notifies you in the app, by email and on mobile when you're assigned, mentioned, or
a task you follow changes. Change it in **Profile › Notifications**. Team notifications in Slack:
see [Integrations](integrations.md#slack).

## Keyboard shortcuts

`c` creates a task, `/` searches, `g p` goes to projects, `?` shows all shortcuts.
